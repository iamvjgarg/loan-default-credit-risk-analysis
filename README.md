## 🎯 Project Overview

How can banks identify customers who are more likely to default on their loans?

This project explores loan default patterns, customer credit risk, repayment behaviour, and recovery performance using a synthetic Indian banking dataset.

The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful business insights that can help financial institutions improve lending decisions and manage credit risk.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** This project uses synthetically generated data for educational and portfolio purposes. All findings are illustrative and do not represent actual banking or customer data.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- 📊 Analyse the overall loan portfolio.
- 🔍 Identify customer segments with higher default risk.
- 💳 Evaluate repayment and delinquency patterns.
- 🚗 Identify loan categories contributing most to overdue amounts.
- 📉 Understand the relationship between credit scores and loan defaults.
- 💡 Develop data-driven recommendations for credit risk management.

## 📁 Dataset Overview

The project contains four interconnected datasets:

| Dataset | Records | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| 👥 Customers | 50,000 | Customer demographics and employment details |
| 🏦 Loans | 75,000 | Loan amounts, interest rates, tenure and outstanding balances |
| 💰 Repayments | 200,000 | EMI payment history and repayment status |
| ⚠️ Loan Status | 75,000 | Default indicators, DPD and overdue information |

💱 All monetary values are represented in Indian Rupees (INR).

### 🔍 Credit Risk Insights

- 🚗 Commercial vehicle loans recorded the highest default rate at **17.58%**.
- 📉 Customers in the poor credit score category recorded a default rate of **23.70%**.
- 👔 Self-employed customers recorded a default rate of **11.51%**.
- 💰 Default rates increased with higher loan amounts across the analysed loan amount categories.

### 💳 Repayment Performance

### 🚨 Recovery Insights

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the illustrative analysis:

- 🔍 Strengthen credit assessment for applicants with lower credit scores.
- 🚗 Monitor commercial vehicle loans more closely due to higher default rates and overdue exposure.
- 👔 Develop suitable risk assessment strategies for self-employed borrowers.
- 💰 Apply additional credit checks to high-value loans.
- 🚨 Prioritise collection efforts for accounts in higher DPD buckets.
- 📊 Use repayment behaviour to identify early warning signals.

## ⚠️ Project Limitations

- All datasets are synthetically generated.
- Findings are illustrative and may not reflect real-world banking behaviour.
- Recommendations would require validation using actual banking data before operational implementation.
